📋 Data Sources & Limitations

🍁 Toronto, ON:

  • Real-time dispatch data from Toronto Fire Services, Toronto Police, and TTC
  • Updated every 2 minutes from live Computer-Aided Dispatch (CAD) systems
  • 🟢 REAL-TIME - incidents appear within seconds of dispatch

🗽 New York City, NY:

  • Public incident data from FDNY and NYPD via NYC Open Data
  • 🟡 DELAYED - May be delayed 15-60 minutes from actual dispatch
  • Some sensitive calls may be excluded for privacy/security
  • MTA transit alerts are real-time

⚠️ General Disclaimer:

  • All data provided for informational purposes only
  • Not a substitute for calling 911 in an emergency
  • Incident data may be filtered, delayed, anonymized, or incomplete
  • Do not rely on this app as your sole source of emergency information
  • Always follow official emergency service guidance
